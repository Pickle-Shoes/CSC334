About Me

Hello, my name is Luke Martin and I am a student at DSU majoring in computer science. Although I spend a lot of my time around technology, I like to go on trips and spend time outside. One of my favorite trips I have taken in my life was to Canada. I went to go visit some relatives in Calgary. While there, we went hiking in the mountains at Banff. I had never been to a mountain before so being on top of one was an awesome feeling, and an even better view. I hope to go back sometime soon and take more trips where I can hike mountains!
